# Relay logo brief

Relay is a video workflow workspace designed first for freelance video editors. It helps each edit move from brief to final delivery.

## Direction

- Build an abstract mark that can survive a product rename.
- Create a simple, recognizable software logo. People do not need to decode a deeper meaning from it.
- Use forward progress and convergence only as construction prompts, not as a story the audience must understand.
- Make the identity feel dependable first, then fast.
- Aim for bold technology rather than cinematic luxury or quiet utility.
- Use a fully monochrome identity with no fixed accent color.
- Build the symbol from three measured geometric curves moving left to right into one compact solid form, using negative space to separate the paths.
- Keep the symbol free-standing and use controlled asymmetry.
- Avoid literal play buttons, film strips, cameras, timelines, chain links, arrows, and lightning bolts. An `R` monogram may be explored when it feels simple and ownable.
- Do not preserve the old mark for recognition. Relay has no existing users.

## Logo system

- Create a standalone symbol and a Relay wordmark.
- Set the wordmark as `Relay` in a precise modern sans with restrained custom details.
- Prioritize the app icon and favicon, then the marketing site header.
- The symbol must remain clear at 16 pixels.
- The static logo is the master. A short one-time convergence animation may support launches and transitions.

## Brand system

- Overview board: [relay-brand-kit.png](./relay-brand-kit.png)
- Use the SVG as the logo source. The overview board defines the visual system, not logo geometry.
- Palette: true black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`, and zinc `#71717A`
- Typography: Geist Sans with Geist Mono for small technical labels
- Brand line: "From first cut to final handoff."
- Image direction: black-and-white editorial video frames, production details, and controlled motion
- Interface direction: thin rules, square controls, sparse labels, and no decorative card or pill chrome

The marketing homepage must lead with a concise centered promise, show a credible Relay workspace above the fold, and follow it with four short workflow principles. On mobile, reduce the workspace preview to a focused project queue instead of shrinking the desktop dashboard.
